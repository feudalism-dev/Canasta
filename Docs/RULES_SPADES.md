# Spades — partnership v1

`family=trick`, variant `spades`. Fixed partners **1+3 vs 2+4**.

## Deal & bid

- 52-card deck, 13 each.
- Each player bids **0–13** tricks (clockwise from left of dealer). **0 = Nil**.
- Partners’ non-nil bids add to a **team contract**. Nil is scored separately (±100).

## Play

- ♠ is always trump.
- Follow suit if able. Highest trump wins; else highest of the led suit.
- Cannot **lead** a spade until spades are broken (a spade has been played), unless holding only spades.
- Left of dealer leads the first trick.

## Scoring (to 500)

- **Made contract:** +10 × team bid, +1 per overtrick (“bag”).
- **Set:** −10 × team bid (no bags that hand for the contract).
- **Nil made / failed:** +100 / −100 (in addition to partner’s contract result).
- Failed-nil tricks do **not** help the partner’s contract; they still count as bags.
- Every **10 bags** accumulated: −100 (bags reduced by 10).
- First partnership to **500** wins (higher total if both cross).

No blind nil in v1.
