# Classic Skippo

Sequencing parlor game (Skip-Bo style) using numbered cards **1–12** and wild **Skippo** cards.

## Setup

- 2–4 players.
- Deck: twelve of each rank 1–12, plus 18 Skippo wilds (162 cards).
- Each player receives a **stock** of 30 cards (top face-up). Hand starts empty.
- Shared: up to **four building piles**.
- Personal: **four discard piles** each.

## Turn

1. Refill your hand to **5** from the draw pile (reshuffle completed builders when draw empties).
2. Play any legal cards from your **hand**, **stock top**, or **discard tops** onto builders.
3. A builder accepts the next number in order (**1 → 12**). Skippo wilds count as whatever number is needed.
4. Completing **12** clears that builder (cards go to recycle).
5. End your turn by **discarding one hand card** onto one of your discard piles (unless you already emptied your stock and won).

## Winning

- First player to empty their **stock** wins the hand.
- Score opponents’ remaining stock cards: **5** each, Skippo wild **25**, added to the winner’s match total.
- Match play-to: **500**.

## Call-outs

There is no “Uno”-style call. Progress is measured by stock size on the score strip.
