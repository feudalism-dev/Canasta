# Euchre — partnership v1

`family=trick`, variant `euchre`. Fixed partners **1+3 vs 2+4**.

## Deck & deal

- 24 cards: **9–A** in four suits.
- 5 each; **4-card kitty** face-down (top turned for the bid).

## Bidding

- Left of dealer speaks first.
- **Round 1:** order up the turned suit as trump (maker’s partner is the team), or pass. Dealer may take the turned card into hand and discard one if ordered up.
- **Round 2:** if all pass, name any other suit as trump, or pass. If all pass again, redeal.
- Maker may choose **alone** (partner sits out; must take 3+ alone for points).

## Play

- Jack of trump (**right bower**) is highest; other Jack of same color (**left bower**) is second; then A–9 of trump; then off-suit.
- Follow suit if able (left bower counts as trump).
- Maker’s team wins the hand by taking **3+** tricks.

## Scoring (to 10)

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Makers 3–4 tricks | 1 |
| Makers all 5 (march) | 2 |
| Makers alone, 3–4 | 1 |
| Makers alone, 5 | 4 |
| Makers euchred (&lt;3) | Defenders score 2 |

First partnership to **10** wins.
