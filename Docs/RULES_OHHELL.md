# Oh Hell — free-for-all v1

`family=trick`, variant `ohhell`. No partnerships — each player scores alone.

## Deal

- 52-card deck. Hand size cycles **7 → 1 → 7** (repeat) until someone reaches the play-to score.
- One card turned from the unused stock as **trump** for that hand.
- Dealer rotates each hand.

## Bidding

- Left of dealer bids first (0 through hand size).
- **Dealer’s constraint:** total of all bids must **not** equal the hand size (dealer is forced to pick a legal bid).

## Play

- Follow suit if able. Trump wins over off-suit; highest of led suit otherwise.
- Left of dealer leads.

## Scoring (to 50)

- Exact bid: **+10 + tricks taken**.
- Miss: **0** that hand.
- First to **50** wins (highest if several cross together).
