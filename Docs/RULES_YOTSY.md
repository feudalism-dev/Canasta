# Yotsy — Classic rules (this build)

## Goal

Highest total after every player has filled all **13** score boxes.

## Dice

Five standard dice. Each turn you may **roll up to three times**. Between rolls, tap dice to **hold** them. After scoring (or scratching) a box, your turn ends.

## Upper section

Aces through Sixes — score the sum of dice showing that face.  
If upper subtotal reaches **63**, add a **35** bonus.

## Lower section

| Box | Score |
|-----|--------|
| 3 of a Kind | Sum of all dice if ≥3 alike |
| 4 of a Kind | Sum of all dice if ≥4 alike |
| Full House | 25 |
| Small Straight | 30 (sequence of 4) |
| Large Straight | 40 (sequence of 5) |
| YOTSY | 50 (five of a kind) |
| Chance | Sum of all dice |

## Yotsy bonus

If the YOTSY box already shows **50** and you roll another five-of-a-kind, you get **+100** and may place the dice in another open box. Upper of that face scores normally; lower boxes may be used as **jokers** (Full House 25, straights 30/40, etc.).

## Match end

After 13 turns each, highest grand total wins (upper + bonus + lower + Yotsy bonuses).
