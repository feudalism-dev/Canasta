# Bolivia — Rules reference

Bolivia is a Samba derivative: same three-deck base, **groups + sequences**, draw two / discard one, but with **expanded wild-card melds** and a **stricter going-out requirement**. This document follows [Pagat’s Bolivia summary](https://www.pagat.com/rummy/samba.html#bolivia) plus common table rules cited in club and commercial rule sets.

**In this product:** Bolivia is a **beta** game in the selector (playable), sharing Samba’s sequence engine. Rules or scoring may still change before it graduates.

---

## Shared with Samba

Bolivia uses the same deck, deal sizes, turn rhythm, group/sequence meld types, card point values, initial-meld table, discard-pile sequence extension, and red-three blocking behavior as Samba unless noted below.

See [RULES_SAMBA.md](./RULES_SAMBA.md) for full detail on sequences, sambas, canastas, and pile pickup.

---

## Players, deck, deal

- **162 cards** (three decks + six jokers).
- **15 cards** each (2–5 players); **13** with six players.
- **2 or 4 players** for our table (same as Samba).

---

## Wild cards — main difference from Samba

In Samba, wilds only appear in **groups**, with tight limits (max 2 wilds, 2× naturals rule).

In Bolivia:

- Wilds may be melded **freely** in groups (subject to minimum meld size).
- You may start a meld of **three or more wild cards only** (no naturals required).
- A completed meld of **seven wild cards** (twos and/or jokers) is called a **Bolivia** (also **wild book** in some books).
- **Bolivia bonus:** **+2,500** (Pagat; some house rules use 2,000).
- **No further cards** may be added once the seven-card Bolivia is complete (closed wild book).

There is **no** Samba-style “at most 2 wilds per group” cap; group/canasta wild limits follow Bolivia’s looser wild-meld rules instead.

**Discard pile:** If the top discard is a **wild** (two or joker), the next player **cannot take the pile** (stricter than Samba, where a wild top only blocks but rank pickup might still be discussed for partial cases — Pagat Bolivia explicitly disallows pile take on wild top).

---

## Groups and canastas

- Groups of equal rank behave as in Samba, but with Bolivia wild flexibility.
- **Canasta** = seven or more in a group; pure/mixed bonuses typically remain **+500 / +300** (same as Samba).
- Multiple same-rank groups and merging two groups into one canasta still apply (Samba rule).

---

## Sequences and sambas (escaleras)

Unchanged from Samba:

- 3–7 naturals, same suit, consecutive rank; no wilds or threes.
- Seven cards = **samba** (+1500); cannot extend after seven.
- Pagat and club rules call a seven-card sequence an **escalera** in Bolivia context.

---

## Black threes

**Pagat / Samba lineage:** Black threes are melded **only when going out**, in a group.

**Common Bolivia house variation:** Black threes may be **melded during play** as ordinary group melds (still no wilds). If this variation is used:

- Each black three **left in hand** at end of hand scores **−100** (not the Samba 5-point deadwood only).

**Recommendation for our game:** Ship Pagat-aligned Bolivia first (black threes on go-out only, 5-point deadwood). Add an optional house toggle later if players request the meld-during-play variant.

---

## Red threes

Same as Samba (optional single meld, +100 each when go-out requirements met, +1000 for all six, penalties if requirements not met, −750 if held at end, block discard when discarded).

Going-out requirements for Bolivia **change when red threes count as bonus** — team must satisfy Bolivia’s meld requirements, not Samba’s “two sambas or two canastas or one each” (see below).

---

## Initial meld

Same score-based table as Samba. Notable club rule: minimum stays **150** from **7,000+** team score (same top tier as Samba).

| Team score at start of hand | Minimum |
|----------------------------|---------|
| Negative | 15 |
| 0 – 1,495 | 50 |
| 1,500 – 2,995 | 90 |
| 3,000 – 6,995 | 120 |
| 7,000+ | 150 |

---

## Going out — stricter than Samba

Pagat Bolivia:

> To go out, **two 7-card melds** are needed, of which **at least one must be a sequence meld (escalera)**.

So the team needs **two completed seven-card melds** among:

- Samba (sequence),
- Canasta (group),
- Bolivia (wild book),

and **at least one** must be a **samba / escalera**.

**Examples that qualify:**

- 1 samba + 1 canasta
- 1 samba + 1 Bolivia
- 2 sambas

**Examples that do not qualify:**

- 2 canastas only (no sequence)
- 1 canasta + 1 Bolivia (no sequence)
- 2 Bolivias only (no sequence)

Partner consent rules match Samba (optional ask).

**Going-out bonus:** **+200** (same as Samba).

---

## Scoring a hand

Same structure as Samba, plus:

| Bonus meld | Points |
|------------|--------|
| Samba (escalera) | +1,500 |
| Pure canasta | +500 |
| Mixed canasta | +300 |
| **Bolivia** (7 wilds) | **+2,500** |
| Going out | +200 |
| Red threes | Per Samba rules |

Deadwood: card values as Samba; if using meld-during-play black threes, **−100** per black three in hand at end.

**Match goal:** **15,000** points (typical; Pagat mentions this in the Bolivia section; Samba plays to 10,000).

---

## Samba vs Bolivia (summary)

| Topic | Samba | Bolivia |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Wilds in groups | Max 2; 2× naturals rule | Flexible; wild-only melds OK |
| 7 wild meld | Not a named bonus | **Bolivia** +2,500 |
| Wild on discard top | Blocks pickup | Blocks pickup (explicit) |
| Go out | 2 sambas OR 2 canastas OR 1 each | **2 seven-card melds**, ≥1 must be sequence |
| Play to | 10,000 | 15,000 |
| Black 3 in hand | 5 each (deadwood) | 5 (Pagat) or −100 (house meld variant) |

## Related variants (out of scope)

Pagat and other sources describe extensions we are **not** planning initially:

- **Sambola** — Samba + New Zealand Jonola features
- **Black Samba** — wilds allowed in sequences
- **Trey / Quad** — 4p/6p Bolivia with 3 or 4 decks
- **Royal Canasta** — red/black three “royal” melds
